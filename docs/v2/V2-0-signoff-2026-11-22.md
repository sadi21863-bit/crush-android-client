## 4. What acceptance means — and does not

Accepting this ledger means:

- every brief area has a status and an evidence pointer (the V2-0 exit
  criteria), and
- the open rows above are **named carry-over work**, not hidden or disputed.

It does **not** mean:

- the carried gaps are fixed;
- tablet/wide, extended rail ≥840dp, API 30+, reduced motion, density or
  2.0x rows were measured;
- Onboarding key entry/save/delete ran on device (owner credential only);
- release blockers are cleared — yolo permission default, exposed-key
  rotation and `SecureKeyStore` coverage stay in V2-9 scope.
